Bring your own addons.

Orbit speaks the open Stremio addon protocol. Paste a manifest link or a stremio:// link from any addon's page, or sign in to Stremio and import the addons you already use.

Before anything is saved, Orbit shows you what the addon provides. Its answers are checked and size-capped, and links that carry your keys are encrypted on your PC.

Private by design.

Orbit treats every addon's answer as untrusted and keeps your accounts on your computer.

Keys

Your keys stay in your OS keychain

Debrid keys and sign-ins live in Windows Credential Manager, never in plain text and never in a link Orbit builds.

Addons

Every answer is checked

Responses are validated, size-capped and timed out. An addon's settings page opens in your own browser, never inside Orbit, and Orbit never runs an addon's code.

History

Pause your history any time

One switch keeps what you play out of Continue Watching and out of every service you've connected. No analytics, no telemetry, no account to make.

Does Orbit come with movies or shows?

No. Orbit is a player. It shows what the addons and services you add provide, the same way a web browser shows the sites you visit.

Do my Stremio addons work?

Yes. Orbit uses the same open addon protocol, so any addon installs with its link, and you can import the ones already on your Stremio account.

Is it really free?

Yes, and the source code is public under GPL-3.0. There are no ads and no accounts to make.

Will it play 4K HDR?

Orbit plays through mpv, with picture settings for weaker PCs and for the best picture, HDR output, and audio passthrough where your setup supports it.

Can I use a controller?

Yes. Every screen works with arrows, a remote or a gamepad, and the Couch Look makes it readable from across the room.

Which languages does it speak?

Nineteen, from English and Español to 日本語 and العربية, following your system language. Translations are open to anyone who wants to improve one.

Where do I report a problem?

Open an issue on GitHub. Security problems go through the security policy instead.
